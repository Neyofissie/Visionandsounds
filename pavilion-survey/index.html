Repair priorities, in the report's order

  1. Close the open south-west roof and gableImmediate. Stop water reaching the stage, re-flash the gable and splice-repair rotten purlin ends to restore roof tying to the south wall.
  2. Annex roof and upper floorActive leaks and dry rot. Strip finishes for re-assessment; the roof may need full replacement with new flashings and gutters.
  3. Steel in the outside wallsRepair, plate where needed and re-protect corroding beams and columns, worst along the east wall.
  4. Investigate the west wall crackLikely steel expansion from Annex water ingress; the wall junction may need re-tying.
  5. Ventilation, heating and overall weathertightnessCheck and overhaul all flashings, cladding and parapets; tackle condensation and mould.
  6. Specialist checksTimber rot at the gallery corners and eaves rafter ends; possible asbestos ceiling tiles in the projection room.

The building

Built
1920, John Fairweather, for the George Green cinema chain
Listing
Category C, LB51105 (added 27 May 2008)
Uses
Picture house, later bingo hall
Structure
Steel frame with brick infill bracing; steel roof trusses spanning the full auditorium, timber purlins at close centres
Floors
Timber joists on dwarf walls and steel beams; concrete and timber in the basement
Annex
Two-storey brick block to the west, cut timber roof
Foundations
Assumed mass concrete; no sign of movement or settlement
Client
Board of Trustees, RCCG Solid Rock Edinburgh
Engineer
Ben W Adam CEng, Narro Associates
